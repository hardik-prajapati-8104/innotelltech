Web Development

We build web applications that don’t just look great — they perform at scale, convert visitors into customers, and give your team a platform they can actually build on. From SaaS products to enterprise portals, we engineer for the long term.

What’s Included

Every web development engagement covers the full lifecycle: from discovery and architecture through to launch, load testing, and post-launch optimisation. Here’s what you get:

  • Custom Front-End Development — Pixel-perfect, accessible interfaces built with React or Next.js. WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as standard.
  • Scalable Back-End Architecture — RESTful and GraphQL APIs, built on Node.js or Python. Designed to handle 10x your current traffic without rearchitecting.
  • Database Design & Optimisation — PostgreSQL, MySQL, or MongoDB schema design with query profiling and indexing strategies baked in from day one.
  • Third-Party Integrations — Stripe, HubSpot, Salesforce, Segment, and 50+ other platforms integrated cleanly with documented API contracts.
  • CI/CD & Cloud Deployment — Automated pipelines on GitHub Actions or GitLab CI, deployed to AWS, GCP, or Vercel. Zero-downtime releases by default.
  • Performance & Security Audit — Lighthouse scores, Core Web Vitals optimisation, OWASP Top 10 review, and penetration testing before every launch.

Our Process

We follow a tight, battle-tested process that eliminates surprises and keeps every stakeholder aligned — from kick-off through to production sign-off.

  1. Discovery & Scoping (1–2 weeks) — We map your goals, users, and constraints. Output: a detailed technical specification, phased roadmap, and fixed-price proposal.
  2. Design & Prototype (2–4 weeks) — High-fidelity Figma prototypes reviewed and signed off before a single component is built. No surprises in QA.
  3. Agile Build (6–14 weeks) — Two-week sprints with weekly demos. You see real progress — not slide decks — at every checkpoint.
  4. QA, Launch & Handover — Cross-browser and device testing, load testing at 3x expected peak, and a full technical handover with documentation your team will actually read.
